TREASURY REGISTER — WHAT IT IS TODAY
=====================================


OBJECTIVE
---------

Give a corporate treasurer one screen to run the day: see cash and
exposure, place new deposits, hedge foreign-currency receivables,
respond to breaches, and act on advice — all under one policy, all
recorded to an audit trail, all filtered through the same six checks
that decide whether any deal may book.

The system says no to a trade that breaks policy before it says yes to
a trade that fits. AI helps interpret and prioritise, but it never
books, never predicts markets, and never invents a number.


WHO IT IS FOR
-------------

The primary persona is the corporate treasurer of a mid-cap group.
Around them:

- Analyst — proposes deals up to a small threshold
- Head of Treasury — signs mid-size deals
- CFO — signs the largest, and holds override authority
- Compliance officer — reads the audit trail, never mutates
- Administrator — manages users, roles, workspace settings


CORE FEATURES TODAY
-------------------

1. ONE SURFACE FOR THE TREASURER
   Everything lives on a single screen. Bottom strip navigates between
   eight areas: Exposure, Performance, Hedging, Queue, Breaches,
   Advisory, Ratings & Policy, and Control. One panel opens at a time
   over the surface — no page reloads, no back button, no getting lost.

2. THE SIX-CHECK GATE
   Every deal — deposit, FX forward, planner allocation — passes
   through the same six controls before it can book:
     - Counterparty is approved and active
     - Instrument is permitted for this counterparty
     - Entity limit is not exceeded
     - Group (parent-company) limit is not exceeded
     - Tenor is inside the rating band's ceiling
     - Concentration stays below the policy cap
   Every check runs on every keystroke; the treasurer sees the six
   result pills live before they ever click a button.

3. DEPLOY IDLE CASH (THE PLANNER)
   Given the day's uninvested cash, the planner proposes up to three
   deployment plans, each fully checked and ready to book with one
   click:
     - Blended plan — the treasurer's investment principles applied
       exactly
     - Higher yield — bends lower-rated caps to chase yield
     - Tighter concentration — spreads across at least six names
   The treasurer sets caps (max 50% AAA, max 30% AA, etc.) and the
   system picks the highest-yielding spread inside those ceilings.
   AI writes a short executive summary; the numbers are ours.

4. FX EXPOSURE & HEDGING
   For overseas receipts in EUR / USD / CHF, the system shows:
     - Forecast exposure by currency and time bucket
     - How much is already hedged, how much remains exposed
     - GBP equivalent for board-level conversations
     - Gap to the treasurer's policy target ("USD 40% hedged vs 75%
       target — $21m short")
   Initiate Hedge opens a modal that pre-checks the trade against the
   same six controls, shows an indicative forward rate labelled as
   such, and lets the treasurer book it in one click.
   AI briefs the shape of the exposure; it does NOT predict rates.

5. COUNTERPARTY & POLICY CONTROL
   Every counterparty carries a rating, limits, and instrument
   permissions — all editable, all versioned. Policy changes create
   new versions rather than overwriting old ones, so a check that
   passed six months ago can be re-derived exactly.

6. INVESTMENT PRINCIPLES & FX POLICY
   Treasurer-editable in the Control panel:
     - Rating floor and tenor ceiling for the planner
     - Per-name and per-group concentration caps
     - Rating-band caps (the "buckets")
     - Per-currency hedge policy targets

7. ADVISORY & BREACHES
   An overnight run ranks candidates the treasurer might act on today
   and writes a short brief. Breaches are surfaced in their own panel,
   with an unwind or acknowledge path per breach.

8. RATINGS & CREDIT SIGNAL SCANNER
   Reads seeded news items per counterparty and classifies materiality
   ("watch", "material", "immaterial"). Suggests actions like
   "put on watch" or "reduce" — the treasurer decides.

9. ACCOUNTING EVENTS
   Every lifecycle stage (proposed, active, matured, settled) can fire
   an accounting event routed through the Treasury Register Connector
   into Fusion AHCS, Fusion GL, Oracle EBS, or a custom REST endpoint.
   The routing is editable from Control.

10. EVIDENCE ON EVERY DEAL
    Every booked deal carries a check_run row with the six inputs, the
    policy version in force at booking, the limit version referenced,
    and the person who signed. Re-derives on demand for audit.

11. ADMIN PAGE (NEW)
    Persona-scoped page for user + role + tenant management. Invite
    users with a temporary password, edit roles, disable accounts,
    reset passwords. Every action is written to the audit trail.


AI — WHAT IT DOES AND WHAT IT DOESN'T
-------------------------------------

AI helps in four places today:
  - Planner executive summary (four bullets that pick a recommended
    plan and say why)
  - FX briefing paragraph (ranks currencies by policy-gap severity,
    surfaces watch callouts about concentration or refinance walls)
  - FX hedge governance advisor (comments on concentration, headroom,
    maturity ladder as the treasurer builds a hedge)
  - Credit signal classifier (reads news, flags material items)

AI hard rules:
  - Never predicts, forecasts, or comments on where any FX rate will
    move
  - Never invents a counterparty
  - Never invents a monetary amount
  - Every path has a deterministic fallback — the system works with
    or without an AI key
  - The treasurer always clicks the button; AI never books
